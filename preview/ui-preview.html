Expense Tracker — palette + theme system

Colours now come from src/theme.ts as three tokens per accent: bright (icons/text on dark), deep (fills — guaranteed contrast with white), and soft. Surfaces are neutral, so the accent is the only saturated thing on screen.

Why the palette changed

Before bg surface elevated primary 2.3:1 Surfaces were green-tinted, so teal-on-teal-on-teal left nothing to pop — and white text on #14B8A6 lands at 2.3:1, under the 4.5:1 (even 3:1) requirement.
After bg surface elevated 10.1:1 5.4:1 Neutral surfaces with a slight blue cast match the slate text tokens; the bright accent is for text/icons (10.1:1) and the deep accent for fills (5.4:1).

Implemented in the app: ThemeProvider / useTheme() / useThemedStyles() with the choice persisted to AsyncStorage; every screen and component builds its StyleSheet from the active theme, so switching takes effect instantly with no reload. The Settings screen has the Mode (Auto / Light / Dark) and six-accent picker shown above. Filled surfaces (hero, FAB, primary buttons, and the green save/import buttons) use the deep variants, which is what brings the previously failing white-on-colour contrast up to 5.4:1 and 5.1:1.
